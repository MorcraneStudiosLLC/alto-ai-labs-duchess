# Public Evidence Boundary

This public-facing package may contain:

- aggregate benchmark results;
- task definitions and scoring descriptions;
- sanitized methodology summaries;
- public-safe architecture diagrams;
- reproducibility metadata that does not expose protected assets;
- research status and limitations;
- links to public Morcrane research articles.

It must not contain without explicit owner release authorization:

- Duchess model weights;
- private source code;
- proprietary orchestration;
- internal system prompts;
- credentials or secrets;
- private training datasets;
- sealed holdout/gold labels that would compromise future evaluation;
- confidential partner/customer data;
- unpublished internal project text;
- artifacts whose release would impair patent, copyright, trade-secret, contractual, or evaluation controls.

## Evidence rule

A result may be presented as measured only when its source artifact, denominator, scorer, version, and provenance are preserved.

A research direction may be presented aggressively as a destination, but it must not be presented as an already measured capability.

# ALTO AI Labs — Research Program

## Thesis

ALTO AI Labs investigates **purpose-bounded intelligence**: systems designed to become exceptionally capable at a defined class of work rather than maximizing unrelated breadth.

Duchess is the principal process-intelligence research program.

## Research themes

1. Process-state interpretation
2. Evidence-grounded state and outcome extraction
3. Routing and task classification
4. Planning and execution structure
5. Monitoring, control, and closure
6. Governed memory and retention
7. Deterministic evaluation and promotion gates
8. Resource-efficient local intelligence
9. Failure-aware evaluation
10. Developmental intelligence through qualified experience

## Governing idea

ALTO builds trust through preserved evidence, clear authority boundaries, explicit state, reproducibility, and controlled progression.

Earlier CL1.0 / PGM work established governed cell-block behavior and independent qualification gates, carrying that evidence-centered engineering discipline into ALTO research.

## Destination

The ALTO program is building toward systems that can understand work as process:

**Initiating → Planning → Executing → Monitoring & Controlling → Closing**

The program advances this direction through versioned experiments with explicit measurement contracts.
